Outsourcing Introduction


Mazagon Dock Shipbuilders Limited, is one of India’s leading DPSU shipyard currently engaged in prestigious projects both for Indian Navy and for export market. MDL is building Stealth Frigates, Destroyers and Submarines for Indian Navy.

With the ever-increasing demand for Naval Ships and Commercial Ships with stringent quality & delivery schedules, MDL has embarked upon outsourcing of jobs on a large scale. MDL has a reliable mechanism for Outsourcing of activities in the form of clearly laid down and transparent purchase procedures with active support of established contractors. However, MDL expects to further expand & establish large vendor base. The training to their personnel would be arranged suitably through special training programmes, particularly on safety aspects, etc.
