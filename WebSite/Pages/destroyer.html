MDL has constructed a variety of ships both for the defence and the commercial sector. The first modern warship to be built by the company was the Leander Class frigate "INS NILGIRI". The NILGIRI was launched in October, 1968 and commissioned in 1972. During the next nine years Mazagon Dock built and delivered five more frigates in this class for the Indian Navy.

DELHI CLASS
3 Nos
(1997-2001)

The Delhi-class vessels are the third-largest warships designed and built in India, after the Kolkata-class destroyers and the Shivalik-class frigates. They were built by Mazagon Dock Limited in Mumbai.
